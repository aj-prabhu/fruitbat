# Privacy

Nothing you read leaves your device. No telemetry.

This file will be generated from `spec/network.json` (packet S1-12).

## Outbound traffic

1. Downloading pinned model files from Hugging Face Hub
2. Bug reports you preview and submit via GitHub
3. (macOS only) Optional update checks, disclosed in onboarding and configurable

All summarization and synthesis happens on your machine. No reading history is sent anywhere.

The web app runs entirely in your browser. The macOS app never posts keystrokes or writes the clipboard.

For the canonical list, see `spec/network.json` once drafted.
